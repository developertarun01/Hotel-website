ABOUT US

COMPNAY NAME

The newest addition to hotelier and restaurateur Jeff Klein’s JK Hotel Group, 850 SVB® is a new hotel concept inspired by European residential-style properties where the comforts and accoutrements of home blend seamlessly with the luxury and service of a contemporary hotel.

Nestled on a beautiful street in West Hollywood, the historic building’s circa 1918 bungalow façade leads to a newly designed property developed by architect Marc Appleton and designer Rita Konig in partnership with Klein.

The result is a decidedly different urban getaway. An under the radar pied a terre for the curious and discerning. And a hotel unlike any other in Los Angeles.

SHOPPING

Hotel 850 SVB is located in a residential West Hollywood setting near the Pacific Design Center with close proximity to iconic boutique shopping on Melrose and Robertson.

DINING

The best of LA dining is at your doorstep, with Santa Monica Boulevard’s trendy restaurant scene only steps away. Hotel 850 SVB is also in close proximity to the infamous Sunset Boulevard as well as Beverly Hills and Century City.

CULTURE

The burgeoning art scene, the buzz of the Sunset Strip, the allure of Hollywood’s storied landmarks; there’s always something new to discover.
