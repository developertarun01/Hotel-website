ABOUT US

FAREMATCHPRO

As the latest venture from renowned hotelier and restaurateur Jeff Klein under the JK Hotel Group, Farematchpro® introduces a fresh hospitality vision inspired by elegant European-style residences where the coziness of home naturally intertwines with the sophistication and service of a modern luxury hotel.

Situated along a charming West Hollywood street, the property’s historic 1918 bungalow exterior opens into a reimagined interior created by architect Marc Appleton and designer Rita Konig in collaboration with Klein, resulting in a refined yet welcoming retreat.

The outcome is a truly distinctive urban escape — a discreet haven for the thoughtful and adventurous traveler, and a one-of-a-kind hotel experience in the heart of Los Angeles.

SHOPPING

Set within a quiet West Hollywood neighborhood near the Pacific Design Center, Farematchpro offers effortless access to the celebrated boutique shopping destinations along Melrose and Robertson.

DINING

Experience Los Angeles’ vibrant culinary culture just moments away on Santa Monica Boulevard, home to an array of sought-after restaurants. Farematchpro also sits close to the legendary Sunset Boulevard, as well as Beverly Hills and Century City.

CULTURE

From the thriving local art scene and the excitement of the Sunset Strip to Hollywood’s timeless landmarks, there’s always a new experience waiting to be explored.
