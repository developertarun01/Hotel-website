ROOMS & SUITES

ANYTHING BUT STANDARD

Accommodations at Hotel 850 SVB are bright, and beautifully equipped. Guest rooms feature custom Rita Konig interiors, original artwork from our private collection, hardwood floors, luxury linens, marble bathrooms with shower and bespoke bath amenities, complimentary mini bar snacks with refrigerator, and a cozy work area with desk and chair. Select rooms have a private fireplace.
