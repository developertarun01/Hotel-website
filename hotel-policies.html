HOTEL POLICIES

CHECK IN & CHECK OUT

Check-in begins at 3:00 PM, and check-out is scheduled for 12:00 PM.

VALET PARKING

Our 24-hour valet parking service is available at a daily rate of $55 for your convenience.

PETS

Farematchpro welcomes pets with open arms. A one-time, non-refundable fee of $100 applies for pets weighing 40 lbs or less. Additional cleaning or damage repair costs, if any, are not included in this fee.

SMOKING

To maintain a healthy and pleasant environment, Farematchpro enforces a strict no-smoking policy across all guest rooms and hallways. A $500 fee will be charged if smoking occurs inside a room.

AMENITY FEE

An amenity fee of $25 per day, per room, applies and includes the following benefits:

  • Complimentary continental breakfast each morning in our Living Room
  • Evening happy hour featuring complimentary wine and beer in the Living Room or on the Roof Top Terrace
  • High-speed internet connectivity
  • Complimentary snacks provided in your minibar
  • Filtered water available in every room
  • Complimentary espresso and tea service offered in the Living Room
  • Nightly turndown service with amenities
  • Unlimited local and domestic phone calls

GUEST ROOM POLICIES

All Farematchpro guest rooms are intended for single or double occupancy unless otherwise authorized by management. Parties are strictly prohibited. A maximum of four (4) guests may occupy a room at any time. Outside alcoholic beverages are not allowed on the premises. Photography and videography are prohibited anywhere within the property unless approved in advance by the General Manager.
