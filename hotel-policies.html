HOTEL POLICIES

CHECK IN & CHECK OUT

Check in time is 3pm, Check out time is 12pm.

VALET PARKING

Valet parking is available 24 hours a day at a daily rate of $55.

PETS

Hotel 850 SVB is pet friendly. We charge a one-time, $100 non-refundable fee to guests accompanied by pets 40lbs and under. This does not include any charges associated with extra cleaning or damages in the room caused by a pet.

SMOKING

Hotel 850 SVB has a strict No Smoking policy in all guest rooms and corridors. A $500 damage fee will be charged in the event that a room has been smoked in.

AMENITY FEE

A $25 amenity fee will be charged per day / per room and includes:

  • Complimentary Continental Breakfast each morning served in our Living Room
  • Complimentary Wine and Beer happy hour each evening in the Living Room or Roof Top Terrace
  • High Speed Internet Access
  • Complimentary snacks stocked in your minibar
  • Complimentary filtered water in your room
  • Complimentary Espresso and Tea service in the Living Room
  • Nightly Turndown Amenity
  • Complimentary Local & Domestic Calls

GUEST ROOM POLICIES

All rooms at Hotel 850 SVB are single or double occupancy unless otherwise noted or cleared by management. We have a strict no party policy. A maximum of four (4) guests is allowed at anytime in guest rooms. Outside alcohol is not permitted. Photography or filming is not allowed anywhere on hotel property unless approved by the General Manager.
