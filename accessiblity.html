ACCESSIBLITY

Farematchpro is devoted to offering exceptional hospitality to all our guests and proudly ensures full ADA compliance for their convenience and comfort.

Accessibility Assistance

If you encounter any issues while browsing or accessing a part of our website, please reach out to us immediately at contact@farematchpro.online. Our team will gladly assist you in obtaining any needed information and support. We are fully dedicated to keeping our website accessible for every visitor and will promptly address any limitations or concerns that arise.

Goal of Accessibility for Everyone

Our primary mission is to create a smooth, inclusive experience for all—both on our website and at our property. We continuously strive to ensure equal access to every part of our hotel and digital platforms for every guest.

Digital Partner Commitment

Farematchpro utilizes the Synyxsis booking platform for online reservations. We’re honored to collaborate with Synyxsis, as they also uphold strong accessibility standards and are actively aligned with modern compliance requirements.

Taking Action & Ongoing Efforts

Farematchpro conducts regular accessibility audits through live user testing and actively makes improvements to ensure that our website serves everyone—including users who rely on screen readers, magnifiers, voice commands, captions, alternative text, and other assistive tools. We continue to update our website promptly and welcome any immediate feedback at contact@farematchpro.online if accessibility challenges are experienced.

Feedback

We value your input. Please contact us at contact@farematchpro.online with any ideas, feedback, or suggestions on how we can further improve the accessibility of our website for all users.

Accessibility Features at Farematchpro

We provide essential ADA-compliant amenities for all guests with accessibility needs. Accessible rooms may be reserved through our website, by phone at +424.323.8650, or by emailing contact@farematchpro.online.

Property Accessibility

  • Valet services available for vehicles requiring accessibility accommodations.
  • Wheelchair-accessible hotel entrance and pathways connecting public restrooms and shared guest spaces.
  • Seating arrangements available for wheelchair users upon reservation.
  • All areas reachable via wheelchair-accessible elevators.
  • Personalized assistance for guests with special requirements.

Service Animals

Registered Service Animals are always welcome at no additional cost. Guests are, however, responsible for any damages that may occur within the room due to the animal’s presence.

ADA Guest Rooms include:

  • Wheelchair-friendly entryways and interior layouts allowing easy mobility around the room without obstruction.
  • Roll-in shower equipped with seating.
  • Lowered storage areas, safes, minibars, closet rods, and beds for easier access.
  • Thermostats and light controls placed at a reachable, unobstructed height.
  • Televisions equipped with Closed Captioning.
  • Telephone with TTY device available.

Additional ADA Compliant Property Amenities:

  • Guest wheelchair available upon request.
  • Shower chair available for additional support.
  • Hearing and visual alert systems (vibration and light signals) for guests who are blind or hard of hearing.
  • Braille signage throughout the property.
