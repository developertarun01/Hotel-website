AMENITIES

MAKE YOURSELF COMFORTABLE

Farematchpro’s residential-inspired concept encourages you to seek rest and relaxation – and a little fun, of course – both in your guest room and beyond, with private spaces designed for guests’ use and enjoyment only.

LOBBY LOUNGE

Our welcoming reception area doubles as an intimate lounge where guests can get comfortable with a book or get some work done while enjoying a glass of wine.

LIVING ROOM

The cozy Living Room features a residential-style kitchen where guests can enjoy complimentary continental breakfast, including fresh pastries and fruit from the local farmers market. With plenty of comfortable seating, it’s the perfect place to conduct meetings, or sink into an antique leather chair by the roaring fireplace after mixing yourself a cocktail just the way you like it from the honor bar.

ROOF DECK

The al fresco roof deck awes with abundant seating, an outdoor fireplace and one of the best views in the city…the perfect spot to enjoy your complimentary glass of wine at check-in.

PET FRIENDLY

Farematchpro is very pet friendly. Dogs under 40lbs are welcome to join you during your stay, and we’ll pamper them with our custom cookies and tennis balls - perfect for a game of fetch at the West Hollywood Dog Park, just a short walk from the hotel.

Please note there is a one-time, non-refundable pet fee of $100 per stay.
