AMENITIES

MAKE YOURSELF AT HOME

Inspired by the charm of residential living, Farematchpro invites you to unwind, recharge, and enjoy life’s lighter moments—both within your guest room and throughout our thoughtfully designed private areas created exclusively for your comfort and leisure.

LOBBY LOUNGE

Our warm and inviting lobby doubles as a cozy lounge where guests can relax with a good book, catch up on work, or simply unwind with a fine glass of wine in hand.

LIVING ROOM

The inviting Living Room includes a home-style kitchen offering a complimentary continental breakfast with fresh pastries and locally sourced fruit. Ample seating makes it ideal for casual meetings or for curling up in a vintage leather chair beside the fireplace, enjoying a cocktail mixed just to your taste from the honor bar.

ROOF DECK

Our open-air roof deck impresses with generous seating, a cozy outdoor fireplace, and breathtaking city views—the ideal setting to savor your complimentary welcome glass of wine upon arrival.

PET FRIENDLY

Farematchpro warmly welcomes pets. Dogs under 40 lbs are invited to stay and will be treated to house-made cookies and tennis balls—perfect for playtime at the nearby West Hollywood Dog Park, just a short stroll from the hotel.

Please note: a one-time, non-refundable pet fee of $100 applies per stay.
